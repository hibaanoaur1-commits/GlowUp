Period & Health 🩸💗

Learn about your menstrual cycle, how to sync your lifestyle, relieve cramps, and take care of your body gently. 🌷

1. The 4 Phases of Your Cycle 🌙

Your cycle isn't just your period. It has four distinct phases, each with its own hormonal changes.

1

Menstrual (Days 1–5)

Estrogen and progesterone are at their lowest. You may feel tired and crampy. This is your "winter" phase—rest is key.

2

Follicular (Days 5–13)

Overlaps with your period. Estrogen rises, energy improves, and you feel more creative and motivated.

3

Ovulation (Days 14–16)

Estrogen peaks and an egg is released. You feel your most confident and energetic. Fertility is highest here.

4

Luteal (Days 1–28)

Progesterone rises. You might experience PMS (bloating, cravings). Energy drops as you prepare for the next period.

2. Cycle Syncing: What to Do 🌿

Adjust your lifestyle, food, and workouts to match your cycle phases.

🍲 Food

Eat iron-rich foods (spinach, lentils, beef) to replace lost blood. Warm soups and ginger tea help with cramps.

🧘‍♀️ Movement

Rest! Do gentle yoga, stretching, or go for a slow walk. Don't push yourself to do intense workouts.

🎨 Energy

Reflect, journal, and say "no" to things that drain you. It's a time for self-care.

🍲 Food

Eat fresh, vibrant foods. Salads, lean proteins, and fermented foods (like yogurt) help your body metabolize estrogen.

🏃‍♀️ Movement

Your energy is rising! Try cardio, dancing, or trying a new workout class.

🎨 Energy

Start new projects, brainstorm ideas, and socialize. You are highly creative right now.

🍲 Food

Eat lighter meals, lots of veggies, and fiber. Avoid heavy or processed foods to keep your digestion fast.

🏋️‍♀️ Movement

You have peak energy! Do high-intensity workouts, weight lifting, or HIIT.

🎨 Energy

Schedule important meetings, go on dates, and be social. Your communication skills are at their best.

🍲 Food

You might crave sugar and carbs. Reach for sweet potatoes, dark chocolate, and complex carbs to stabilize your mood.

🚶‍♀️ Movement

Scale back. Pilates, light strength training, or walking are best. Your body is preparing to rest.

🎨 Energy

Nest and organize. Finish up old tasks, clean your space, and get cozy. Don't start huge new projects.

3. Natural Cramp Relief 🍵

Gentle remedies to help you feel better when cramps hit.

🔥 Heat Therapy

Apply a heating pad or hot water bottle to your lower belly. The heat relaxes the uterine muscles and increases blood flow, reducing pain.

🍵 Herbal Teas

Drink ginger, chamomile, or peppermint tea. Ginger has anti-inflammatory properties that can soothe cramps naturally.

💆‍♀️ Gentle Massage

Massage your lower abdomen in a circular motion with a little bit of warm oil. This helps relax the muscles and ease tension.

4. Gentle Care & Knowing When to Ask for Help 🩺

Listen to your body. It's important to know what's normal and what needs a doctor's check.

🚨 When to see a doctor

It's normal to have some discomfort, but you should talk to a healthcare provider if you experience:

Severe Pain: Cramps so bad you have to miss school or work, even with painkillers.

Heavy Bleeding: Soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for more than 2 hours.

Irregular Cycles: Your period is consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

💚 Remember: Every body is different. Never feel embarrassed to ask a doctor about your period!
