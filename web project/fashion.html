Fashion & Outfits 🛍️

Discover your body shape, find aesthetic outfit inspiration, and learn how to combine colors to express your personal style! ✨

1. How to find your Body Shape 📏

Knowing your body shape helps you choose clothes that flatter your natural silhouette.

The Measuring Guide 📐

Grab a measuring tape and measure yourself in front of a mirror:

⏳ Hourglass

Your bust and hips are roughly the same size, with a clearly defined, narrower waist.

🍐 Pear (Triangle)

Your hips are noticeably wider than your bust and shoulders.

🍎 Apple (Round)

Your shoulders and bust are broader, and your waist is less defined.

📏 Rectangle (Straight)

Your bust, waist, and hips are all roughly the same width.

2. What to Wear for Your Shape 👗

Click on any clothing item to see the full picture!

⏳ Hourglass Shape

Highlight your waist and follow your natural curves.

👖 JeansHigh-waisted or bootcut.
👗 SkirtsPencil or wrap skirts.
👚 TopsV-necks or wrap tops.
👘 DressesWrap or belted dresses.

🍐 Pear Shape

Draw attention upward and skim over your hips.

👖 JeansBootcut or straight-leg.
👗 SkirtsA-line or flowy midi.
👚 TopsBoat necks or off-shoulder.
👘 DressesFit-and-flare dresses.

🍎 Apple Shape

Create a waist and highlight your legs.

👖 JeansStraight-leg or bootcut.
👗 SkirtsHigh-waisted A-line.
👚 TopsV-necks or flowy tunics.
👘 DressesEmpire waist or wrap.

📏 Rectangle Shape

Create curves by adding volume.

👖 JeansHigh-waisted flares.
👗 SkirtsPleated or tiered.
👚 TopsPeplum or ruffled tops.
👘 DressesBelted or fit-and-flare.

4. Popular Style Aesthetics 🎭

Find the aesthetic that matches your personality!

🤍 Clean Girl

Minimalist, neat, and polished. Think matching sets, neutral colors, gold jewelry, and slicked-back buns.

🦋 Y2K

Fun, bold, and nostalgic of the 2000s. Think low-rise jeans, baby tees, bright colors, and chunky accessories.

🐎 Old Money

Classic, elegant, and timeless. Think tailored blazers, loafers, pearl earrings, and high-quality fabrics.
