1. How to know your Skin Tone 🎨

Knowing your undertone helps you pick the perfect foundation, blush, and lipstick.

The Vein Test 🩸

Look at the veins on the inside of your wrist under natural light.

❄️ Cool Undertones (Veins look Blue/Purple)

Best colors: Silver jewelry, pink/berry blushes, blue-red lipsticks.

☀️ Warm Undertones (Veins look Green/Olive)

Best colors: Gold jewelry, peach/coral blushes, orange-red lipsticks.

🌿 Neutral Undertones (Mix of both)

Best colors: Rose gold, mauve, and soft peachy-pinks.

Lipstick completely changes your vibe. Here is a guide to finding your perfect shade:

Eyeshadow can be soft or dramatic. If you don't know where to start, try these:

  • Champagne / Taupe: The safest bet.
  • Bronze / Gold: Makes your eyes pop.
  • Soft Plum / Brown: Great for a subtle smokey eye.

2. The Basics: Blush & Eyeshadow 👁️

Learn exactly where to place your colors.

🌸 The Placement Guide

Blush placement changes your whole face shape!

  • Apples of cheeks (Smile & tap): Makes you look youthful and cute.
  • High cheekbones (Sweep up): Lifts the face and gives a model look.
  • Across the nose (Igari style): Creates a sun-kissed, soft girl aesthetic.

✨ The Technique

Always tap off excess powder before applying. Blend upwards towards your temples so it melts into your skin.

🎨 The 3-Shade Rule

Never get confused again! Just use three colors:

  • 1. Light Shade (Base): Apply a light matte color all over your eyelid.
  • 2. Medium Shade (Crease): Apply in the crease (where your eye folds) and blend in windshield-wiper motions.
  • 3. Dark Shade (Outer V): Apply to the outer corner to add depth, and blend!

💡 Pro Tip

Tap your brush! Never drag your brush hard. Use a fluffy brush to blend the edges until there are no harsh lines.

🖌️ The 3-Point Guide

Use a straight makeup brush or pencil to measure your brows:

  • 1. The Start: Hold the brush straight up from the inner corner of your eye. This is where your brow should begin.
  • 2. The Arch: Look straight ahead. Line the brush up from the outer edge of your iris to find where the highest point of your arch should be.
  • 3. The Tail: Angle the brush from the corner of your nose past the outer corner of your eye. This is where your brow should end.

✨ Pro Tip

Always pluck or shave below the brow line, never above it! Fill in sparse areas with light, upward strokes using a brow pencil.

4. Types of Makeup Looks 🎭

Different vibes for different occasions. Look at the examples to find your favorite!

🌿 Natural / Clean Girl

Focuses on glowing, healthy skin. Uses tinted moisturizer, cream blush, brow gel, and lip balm. Perfect for school or everyday wear.

🎀 Soft Girl / Douyin

Very popular right now! Features heavy pink blush, glossy lips, shimmery eyelids, and sometimes little faux freckles. Very cute and dreamy.

🖤 Full Glam / Evening

High coverage foundation, heavy contour, dramatic smokey eyes or winged eyeliner, false lashes, and a bold lip. For parties and events.
