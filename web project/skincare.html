Skincare & Self-Care 🌿

Discover simple skincare routines, glass-skin basics, and relaxing self-care habits to glow from the inside out. 🧴✨

1. How to find your Skin Type 💧

Before buying products, you need to know what your skin needs.

The Bare-Face Test 🧼

Wash your face, pat it dry, and do not apply any products. Wait 30 minutes and check how it feels:

🌿 Normal

Feels comfortable, balanced, and neither too oily nor too dry. Minimal breakouts. You're lucky!

💧 Oily

Shiny all over, especially on the forehead and nose. Needs lightweight, gel-based products.

🏜️ Dry

Feels tight, flaky, or rough. Needs rich, hydrating creams and gentle cleansers.

🌗 Combination

Oily T-zone (forehead/nose) but normal or dry cheeks. Needs a mix of products.

🌸 Sensitive

Gets red, itchy, or irritated easily. Needs fragrance-free and soothing products.

2. The Ultimate Skincare Routine 🧴

The correct order to apply your products for the best results.

1

Cleanser

Wash away the impurities from sleep. Use a gentle cleanser.

2

Vitamin C Serum

Protects your skin from the sun and brightens your complexion.

3

Moisturizer

Locks in hydration and keeps your skin barrier healthy.

4

Sunscreen (SPF 30+)

Never skip this! Protects against premature aging and dark spots.

1

Cleansing Balm / Oil

Melts away makeup and sunscreen from the day.

2

Water-Based Cleanser

Double cleansing ensures your pores are completely clean.

3

Exfoliator (1-2x a week)

Removes dead skin cells. Don't do this every night!

4

Night Cream / Retinol

Repairs the skin while you sleep. Use a thicker moisturizer.

3. The "Glass Skin" Guide ✨

Click on any picture to see the full image!

1

Double Cleanse

Use an oil cleanser followed by a water-based one to ensure zero impurities are left.

2

Hydrating Toner

Apply a hydrating toner (or essence) while your skin is still slightly damp to lock in moisture.

3

Hyaluronic Acid

This is the secret to glass skin! It holds 1000x its weight in water. Apply it to damp skin.

4

Face Oil

Press a few drops of facial oil into your skin to seal in all the hydration and give a glossy finish.

4. Relaxing Self-Care Habits 🛁

Skincare is more than just products. It's about taking time for yourself.

🛁 Unplug & Soak

Take a warm bath with Epsom salts or a bath bomb. Leave your phone in the other room and just relax.

💧 Hydrate from Within

Drink a big glass of water. Hydration starts from the inside out—no cream can replace drinking water!

🧘‍♀️ Gentle Movement

Do 10 minutes of gentle stretching or yoga before bed to release tension and help you sleep better.
