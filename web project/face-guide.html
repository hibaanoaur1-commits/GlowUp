✨ STEP 1: FIND YOUR FACE SHAPE ✨

How to Know Your Face Shape 🔍

Pull your hair back from your face, look in a mirror, and follow these easy steps to find out your natural shape:

📏 1. Measure Your Proportions

Check which part of your face is the widest (forehead, cheekbones, or jawline) and whether your face is longer than it is wide or equal in width and length.

🪞 2. Check Your Jawline

Is your jaw rounded, sharp and square, or does your face gently taper down to a pointed chin?
